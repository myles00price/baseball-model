2026-08-01THE BACKWARDS DEFENSE LEAK
THE LEAKEvery team-strength rating had the defensive half pointing the wrong way. A defense's rating measures how much it ALLOWS, so a high number means a bad defense - and the model was subtracting the opponent's defensive rating where it needed to add it. In plain terms: facing a terrible defense was being scored as a disadvantage.THE EVIDENCECaught in a review pass, not by the results, which is the worrying part. The broken feature still pointed the right way overall (it correlated +0.15 with the final margin), and a weak signal against a sharp market is exactly what you expect to see anyway, so nothing looked wrong. The giveaway was the comparison to the betting line: a real team-strength rating should track the closing line closely, and this one sat at 0.28 instead of 0.84. After the fix, the model's average error per game dropped from 10.55 points to 9.94 and its probability score improved from .243 to .223.THE FIXDefensive ratings are now added to the opposing offense, with the sign convention written into the code as a comment that says do not simplify this. Ten automated tests now check the direction of every relationship - a bad defense must predict a worse result for its own team, a good offense a better one - so a future sign flip fails loudly instead of silently halving the model. Worth stating plainly: the broken version produced a 57.5% season in 2024, easily the most exciting number this project has produced, and it was noise from a bug. The corrected model's seasons are 50.6, 47.7, 53.4, 51.7 - boring, stable, and real.
