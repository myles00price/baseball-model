HOW TO READ THESE TABLES

MODEL columns are this model's numbers. Each sportsbook column shows that book's newest pregame price for the exact team and line in the row, with the time it was captured and its age when the page was built. Prices more than 6 hours old, or captured for a game that has since tipped off, are labelled as saved historical prices; your browser re-checks this when you open the page. “no sportsbook quote” means no price was on file. BOOK NO-VIG % is the average of each book's two-sided price with the margin removed. Model lean uses current (non-stale) quotes only.

THE ENGINE

Model version nba-0.1-team_only · production variant team-only · data through 2026-10-21 · model run THU OCT 22, 4:58 PM ET.
Each game gets an expected score for both teams from possession-based team ratings (offense and defense per 100 possessions, pace, home court, rest). The spread, total and win probability on this page are all read from that one projection, so they agree with each other. Win % uses the projected margin and its fitted uncertainty; fair ML is that win % as American odds with no margin. The numbers come from team ratings only: the roster estimate below is research and context, not applied to any number on this page. No player props are shown on this page.
