2026-08-20SOCCER GROWS UP FOR KICKOFF - PLAYER SHOTS ON TARGET AND A LEDGER
Two additions that make the soccer tab a full member of the board. First, PLAYER shots on target: each fixture lists the likeliest shooters on both sides with a projection per start and the chance of 1-plus and 2-plus on target, each with its fair price. A player's number comes from his own shot history - every shot he has taken, on target meaning goal or forced save - shrunk toward the league rate, decayed for recency, and adjusted for the opponent's shot suppression, then tied to his club's validated team total. Tested walk-forward over 7,355 player-matches across the last two seasons: projected to land 1-plus 38.5% of the time, it happened 38.2%; it runs a hair hot on 2-plus (12.4% projected, 10.7% happened) and the page says so. Second, THE LEDGER: from matchweek 1 the bottom of the page grades every published number after the whistle - the win/draw/win numbers against the closing line, the over/under calls, whether shots landed inside the published ranges, every player projection against what he actually did, and a flat $100 paper record on any price the model liked by 3% or more. Settles run automatically every Tuesday. Grades are final and misses publish too, same as the MLB ledger. Still reference only: no plays, no stakes, nothing texted.
