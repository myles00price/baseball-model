2026-08-20SOCCER GROWS UP FOR KICKOFF - PLAYER SHOTS ON TARGET AND A LEDGER
Two additions that make the soccer tab a full member of the board. First, PLAYER shots on target: each fixture lists the likeliest shooters on both sides with a projection per start and the chance of 1-plus and 2-plus on target, each with its fair price. A player's number comes from his own shot history - every shot he has taken, on target meaning goal or forced save - shrunk toward the league rate, decayed for recency, and adjusted for the opponent's shot suppression, then tied to his club's validated team total. Tested walk-forward over 7,355 player-matches across the last two seasons: projected to land 1-plus 38.5% of the time, it happened 38.2%; it runs a hair hot on 2-plus (12.4% projected, 10.7% happened) and the page says so. Newly promoted squads have no shot history in the dataset, so they get no player numbers rather than guessed ones. Second, THE LEDGER: from matchweek 1 the bottom of the page grades every published number after the whistle - the win/draw/win numbers against the closing line, the over/under calls, whether shots landed inside the published ranges, every player projection against what he actually did, and a flat $100 paper record on any price the model liked by 3% or more. Settles run automatically every Tuesday. Grades are final and misses publish too, same as the MLB ledger. Still reference only: no plays, no stakes, nothing texted.

2026-08-18NEW ON THE BOARD - SOCCER (PREMIER LEAGUE), REFERENCE NUMBERS ONLY
A soccer tab, up in time for the Premier League opener on Friday. It is a goals model: every club carries an attack and a defence rating fitted on every result since 2015 with recent form weighted more, built on expected goals (shot quality) blended with actual goals. Those two ratings give an expected scoreline, and everything on the card - win / draw / win, double chance, over-under, both teams to score, the three most likely scores - is read off that one score grid, laid beside Pinnacle with the vig removed and the best price from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and Caesars. The rules are the same as MMA: it was tested walk-forward, refit before every match date across six seasons and 2,280 matches, and it did not beat the closing line - log-loss 0.979 against the market's 0.963. We also tried starting from the market's own Friday price and letting the model nudge it; that did not beat the Friday price either. Calibration is honest (it says draw 23%, draws happen 24%), but calibration is not an edge, and we say so on the page. So: reference numbers, no plays, no stakes, nothing texted.
