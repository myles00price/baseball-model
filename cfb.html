2026-09-11CFB: COLLEGE FOOTBALL SPREADS AND TOTALS - THE PAPER CARD ARRIVES
New tab on the board: college football. Every game between two FBS teams gets our own spread and total before kickoff, shown next to Pinnacle, DraftKings and FanDuel exactly as they stood when we logged the card, and every number is graded after the final against the line that actually closed. Before anything went up it was tested walk-forward on the 2023 to 2025 seasons, about 2,400 games with real opening and closing lines, and here is the honest reading. On accuracy the closing spread beats us by a little under half a point per game. On direction, when our number disagreed with the opener the close moved toward us 63 percent of the time, about six tenths of a point per game - direction, not distance: the opener still lands closer to the final line than we do about seven times in ten. Against the closing spread, on leans of two points or more, we were 49 percent, a coin flip. Totals are the market where the past results look best: when we disagreed with the opening total by four points or more we were on the right side 57.5 percent of the time across 466 games (54 percent against the closing total), and the close moved our way 69 percent of the time. That is a lead, not a proof, and it gets tested here in public. So: reference numbers only, a LEAN column that says which side our number favors and by how much, rows marked WIDE when we disagree with the book by ten or more because in the first three weeks of the past three seasons our side went 23-24 in spots like that, and a ledger that grades every published number after the game, misses included. Paper season. Nothing is bet, nothing is texted.
2026-09-11CFB: A CORRECTION TO TONIGHT'S TOTALS NUMBER - WITHDRAWN, SAME NIGHT
A few hours ago this page said totals were the market where our past results looked best: on the right side 57.5 percent of the time against the opening total when we disagreed by four points or more. Tonight's audit found that number was resting on a bad ruler. For the 2024 and 2025 seasons the only archived closing total we had to grade against was a single book's stored snapshot, and it fails basic checks that a real closing line passes - it is compressed at the extremes and barely improves on its own opener. Against a number like that, any method that pulls a total toward the middle looks good: a rule with no model in it at all, bet under when the posted total is unusually high and over when it is unusually low, beats that same stored number just as often as we did, about 55 percent, both overs and unders, exactly the pattern we had been reading as a signal. Where the archive holds real closing totals from DraftKings, Caesars and MGM - about 300 games in 2023 - our totals number is 49 percent, a coin flip. So the totals claim is withdrawn. The spread numbers on this page were checked the same way and the spread benchmark passes those tests, so they stand as stated. Totals stay on the card as reference numbers and will be graded against real closing lines recorded this season, and no claim will be made until they earn one.
