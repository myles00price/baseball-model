HOW TO READ THIS PAGE MODEL / BOOK PRICES / PUSHES

MODEL is the model's probability that the selection wins under full-game rules (overtime and shootout count; the shootout winner is credited one goal). MODEL FAIR is that probability as American odds with no margin; on whole-number lines that can push it is the chance of winning given no push, the same basis as NO-VIG. BOOKS shows prices captured from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and Caesars at the time in the status line — they are a dated snapshot, not live prices, and are marked STALE once they pass 6 hours or puck drop. NO-VIG is the average book price with the margin removed (* = whole-number line, conditional on no push). A difference between MODEL and NO-VIG is not an edge: this model has no goalie, lineup or injury information and has never been tested against book prices.

THE ENGINE

Team attack and defence ratings are updated one day at a time from regulation goals, with home ice and back-to-back effects, and carry 70% into a new season. The 60-minute score is modelled so that ties and late empty-net goals happen as often as they really do, and a tied game is settled by overtime or a shootout. Every market on this page is read off that one score distribution, so the numbers are consistent with each other. Player numbers combine recent ice time, even-strength and power-play scoring rates, the opponent's shots allowed and the team's expected goals.

WHAT IT CANNOT SEE

No starting goalies, lineups or injuries. No roster moves beyond what shows up in games already played. Totals barely separate high-scoring from low-scoring games. Recent seasons have more lopsided scores than the model expects, so favourites covering -1.5 on the road are under-predicted. Player numbers assume the player dresses. Sportsbook settlement rules are assumed to be the standard North American ones and have not been checked book by book.
