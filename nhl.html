THE ENGINE

Team attack and defence ratings are updated one day at a time from regulation goals, with home ice and back-to-back effects, and carry 70% into a new season. The 60-minute score is modelled so that ties and late empty-net goals happen as often as they really do, and a tied game is settled by overtime or a shootout. Every market on this page is read off that one score distribution, so the numbers are consistent with each other. Player numbers combine recent ice time, even-strength and power-play scoring rates, the opponent's shots allowed and the team's expected goals.

WHAT IT CANNOT SEE

Sportsbook settlement rules are assumed to be the standard North American ones and have not been checked book by book.
