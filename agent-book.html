Bankroll management & the path to an agency model

Protect the operating bankroll

Future tickets are capped at 1% of the lower of starting bankroll and current bankroll at cost. Combined open stakes are capped at 40% overall, 10% per sport, and 2% per game. Bets starting more than 24 hours away can use at most 10% overall.

In the sportsbook account, NBA and NFL game lines can use 7.5% per sport, leaving 2.5% of bankroll for props within the 10% sport cap. All bets still share the overall limit. Existing tickets retain their terms; an agent above a limit waits for capacity to reopen.

Earn a research track before scaling

The research screen below is a proposal: 50 settled games across 14 settlement dates, positive settled profit, and a positive conservative estimate of return across games. It is an evidence screen, not proof of skill or an automatic promotion.

  1. Define a candidate. Save the agency’s hypothesis, features, training cutoff, model version, and reasoning for each prediction.
  2. Validate on future games. Keep candidate predictions and paper results separate; compare calibration, returns, and independent closing prices with the existing model.
  3. Review before scaling. Increase an allocation or add agents only after validation, within a funded agency budget. Similar agents share exposure limits.

Current agents use fixed rule-based strategies. Existing descendants received separate simulated starting bankrolls; those grants are not agency profit. New model development and changes to the existing expansion rules are not activated by this research screen.
