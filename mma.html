REFERENCE NUMBERS — NOT PLAYS

This is a decision-support tool, not a pick service. It publishes a predictive distribution of significant strikes landed, to sit beside a posted line. There is no edge calculation, no stake, and nothing here is texted to subscribers.

Walk-forward validated over 31 quarterly refits, 2019–2026: on main events the per-fighter 50% interval covered 51.4% and the 80% interval 83.8% (nominal 50 / 80), with the PIT indistinguishable from uniform. Both fighters share a bout-level pace factor — a hot fight lifts both counts by about a third per standard deviation — so the bout total is wider than adding two independent fighter distributions would give. Finish rates are scaled to the last twelve months of results.

Totals carry a little more slack than the per-fighter lines (their 80% range covers about 82–86% across all bouts). Lean on the per-fighter numbers.

Out of sample: UFC 330 (Aug 15) with the models as they stood beforehand — 24 fighter projections, 16 inside the 50% range, 22 inside the 80%. The two published strike lines (Machado Garry o33.5, Turner o27.5) were priced at 0.49 and 0.54 to go over; both went under.

Calibration is not an edge. Nothing here says these numbers beat a posted price.
